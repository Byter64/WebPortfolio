Modular Input Deck

A customizable control deck for creative workflows and rapid hardware prototyping.

Hardware Design
Year
2026
Role
Industrial design and hardware prototyping

Designed to Be Modular

The input deck combines rotary controls, buttons, and sliders in interchangeable modules. This allows the interface to be adapted to different tasks.

From Assembly to Interaction

The design considers assembly, cable routing, and tactile orientation from the outset. Cyan status indicators carry the portfolio's visual language into the physical product.
