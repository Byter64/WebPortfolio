Sample project

Signal Runner

A fast-paced game concept combining movement, rhythm, and clear visual cues.

Game DesignProgramming
Year
2026
Role
Concept, game design, and prototyping

The Idea

Signal Runner explores how a small number of precise cues can make a fast-paced experience easy to read. Movement and obstacles follow a shared visual rhythm.

The Prototype

The playable prototype focuses on movement, immediate feedback, and short iteration cycles. Systems and visuals were developed together so that every mechanic remains easy to understand.
